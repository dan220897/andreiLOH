Safety

Risk & Trust Concerns

When you buy at auction without expertise, it can be risky. That's exactly why we exist. Our team performs comprehensive inspections before bidding, reviews complete vehicle history (Carfax + AutoCheck), verifies title status, checks for flood/theft/lemon flags, and assesses mechanical and cosmetic condition. You only approve a purchase after seeing our full report. We don't bid on anything that doesn't meet our quality standards.
Our multi-point inspection process is specifically designed to catch hidden damage. We check structural integrity, paint thickness, panel alignment, undercarriage condition, and run diagnostics on electronics and mechanical systems. We also cross-reference all findings with the vehicle history report. If we find issues that weren't disclosed, we flag them immediately.
We verify title status on every vehicle before bidding. By default, we only bid on clean-title vehicles unless you specifically request otherwise. If a vehicle has a salvage or rebuilt title, we clearly disclose this along with the implications for insurance, resale value, and registration in your state.
We provide detailed photos, history reports, and our inspection findings before placing any bids. Due to the nature of auctions (vehicles sell quickly), in-person visits are sometimes possible but not always practical. However, our thorough documentation gives you all the information needed to make a confident decision.
Because we provide detailed documentation before purchase, surprises are extremely rare. However, if the vehicle's condition materially differs from our inspection report, we work with you to resolve the issue. Our goal is long-term client relationships, not one-time transactions, so we always stand behind our work.

Yes. All vehicles are fully insured during transport. Our carrier partners carry comprehensive cargo insurance that covers any damage that might occur in transit. You'll receive proof of insurance coverage with your delivery confirmation.
