Our Story

Built by Car People, for Car Buyers

A3Dynamix was founded in Los Angeles with a simple mission: give everyday buyers access to the same wholesale auto auctions that dealers have used for decades to source inventory at below-market prices.

We saw how much dealers marked up vehicles — often $10,000 to $20,000 above what they paid at auction. We knew there had to be a better way for consumers to buy cars without overpaying.

Today, we've helped hundreds of clients across all 50 states purchase vehicles at auction prices, saving them thousands of dollars on every transaction. Our team handles everything — from finding the right car to delivering it to your door.
