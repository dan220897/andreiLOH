Core Service

Auction Representation

We act as your licensed representative at all major US auto auctions including Copart, IAAI, Manheim, and ADESA. With access to thousands of vehicles daily, we find and bid on exactly what you need.

  • Access to all major auction platforms
  • Professional bidding strategy
  • Real-time communication during auctions
  • Budget-conscious bidding — we never exceed your limit

Quality Assurance

Vehicle Inspection & Verification

Before any bid is placed, we conduct a thorough inspection of every vehicle. We review history reports, check for hidden damage, verify mileage, and assess overall condition to ensure you get exactly what you expect.

  • Carfax and AutoCheck history reports
  • Title status verification (clean, salvage, rebuilt)
  • Mechanical condition assessment
  • Cosmetic damage documentation with photos
  • Flood, theft, and lemon law check

Auto Selection & Consulting

  • Budget optimization strategies

Logistics

Nationwide Vehicle Delivery

We arrange reliable door-to-door vehicle transport anywhere in the continental United States, plus Hawaii and Alaska. Open and enclosed carrier options available for all vehicle types.
